Quick resource estimate

Use this only when you do not have official percentages. It estimates Team 2 resources from revised overs and wickets lost; it is not official.

Estimated R2 will appear here.

What to enter

If the first innings had no rain interruption, Team 1 resources are normally 100%. If Team 2 loses overs, enter Team 2's available resources from your official table/software. The calculator then gives the revised target and par score. If Team 2 is above par, they are ahead; if below par, they are behind.
